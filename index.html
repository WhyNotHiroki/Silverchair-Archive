About the Archive

Silverchair Archive is a project created to preserve the history of Silverchair. The goal is to document albums, songs, concerts, instruments, equipment, interviews and rare information about the band.


Every page aims to separate confirmed information from uncertain details, creating a reliable resource for fans around the world.
